Design a mobile app screen (a single phone screen, full-bleed, shown flat, no device frame) for "קילו בשבוע" (Kilo A Week), a Hebrew-only weight-loss app. All interface text is in Hebrew and the whole layout is right-to-left.

What the product is: a personal method, not a diet. The whole idea is a calorie deficit over weeks. The user weighs himself every morning and every evening, every day, and also before and after things (peeing, pooping, a run, a swim, a bike ride, noting how much he drank during it). From these numbers the app teaches him how his own body works: how many grams he burns overnight while sleeping, how much he gains during the day, what each activity really burns for him, and whether his weekly trend hits the goal of 700 grams to 1 kilo down per week. The daily number goes up and down; what matters is the weekly trend. He also logs food by a photo of the plate or a sentence, and an AI estimates calories and says if it fits the method (no sugar, no bread/pasta/potatoes, no oil, lean protein, vegetables). There is a friendly AI coach to chat with in Hebrew.
