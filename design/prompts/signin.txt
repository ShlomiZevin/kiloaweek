The welcome / sign-in screen of "קילו בשבוע" (Kilo A Week), a Hebrew-only, right-to-left mobile weight-loss app. The first screen a new user sees after opening the link from a presentation about the method. It says what the app does in one line (weigh yourself morning and evening, learn how your body burns, lose about a kilo a week), shows three short promises (daily weighing and night burn, before/after measurements, food with AI calories and a coach), and has one button: "כניסה עם Google". A single phone screen, flat, no device frame. All text Hebrew.
