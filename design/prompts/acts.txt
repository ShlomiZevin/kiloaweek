A matched set of 10 icons for "קילו בשבוע", a Hebrew weight-loss app where the user weighs himself before and after things to learn what each one burns. The icons sit inside small round buttons of an activity picker, so each must read at 48 pixels. The ten, in this order, left to right then the second row: 1 peeing (a toilet), 2 pooping, 3 running, 4 walking, 5 swimming in a pool, 6 riding a bike, 7 strength training with weights, 8 any other activity, 9 the morning weigh-in (sunrise), 10 the evening weigh-in (night). Arranged as a 5 by 2 grid with large equal gaps between them, each icon centered in its cell, on a plain pure white background. No text, no labels, no numbers, no frames around the cells.
